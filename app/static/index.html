Exports

Finished files stay cached in MixMill. Click Download and choose a destination in the Windows Save As dialog; it opens in Downloads by default.

Fast exports are lossless stream copies — a segment starts at the nearest keyframe at or before your mark, which on some files is several seconds early, and runs through your marked end. Precise exports re-encode (1080p/30fps) for frame-exact cuts.
